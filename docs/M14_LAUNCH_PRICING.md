# M14 — WFM Launch Pricing

Status: Initial launch pricing configured October 8, 2026.

## Public launch schedule
- Free — $0/month
- Professional Scouting — $149/month or $1,490/year
- Club — $299/month or $2,990/year
- Data License — custom commercial pricing

Annual subscriptions are priced at ten months of the monthly rate, effectively giving two months free.

## Positioning
Professional Scouting is for independent scouts, analysts and recruitment professionals. Club is for private club/recruitment workflows. Data License is negotiated because permitted use, exports, territory, volume, redistribution and API/data-feed scope can materially change the commercial value and contractual obligations.

## Billing boundary
The pricing catalog is live in WFM, but payment processing is intentionally not connected yet. Commercial access should be activated only through the existing entitlement/licensing workflow until a billing provider is selected and integrated.

## Launch rule
Do not describe an entitlement as proof of a paid subscription until billing is connected or an executed commercial agreement exists.
